## Key Narrative Elements

### The Red Thread
Finance foundation → operational leadership → entrepreneurial drive → strategic transformation. Each role built on the previous one. The MBA is the capstone, not a restart.

### Transformation, NOT Restructuring
This is the most critical narrative pivot. The track record includes turnarounds (P+P, Polak, AVL), but the positioning must emphasize **modernization, digitalization, strategic repositioning, and business model evolution** — not cost-cutting, headcount reduction, or crisis management.

Acceptable framing:
- "Reengineered processes and modernized infrastructure"
- "Pivoted the business model away from automotive dependency"
- "Deployed AI and BI tools in industrial environments"
- "Coordinated the corporate strategy process"

Framing to avoid:
- "Restructured" (use sparingly, never as the lead)
- "Social plans" / "workforce reduction" (omit or euphemize)
- "Crisis management" (replace with "change management" or "transformation")
- "Cost-cutting" (replace with "performance optimization")
